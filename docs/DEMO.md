# Qamar Square — 90-second judging route

1. **0–15 seconds:** Start Mission. Show the 10,000-credit packing budget. Explain that purchases change later choices, but the crew can survive with base supplies. Launch without purchases.
2. **15–45 seconds:** Open the greenhouse, Save Power, preview, confirm and continue. Adjust the heat valve. Prepare Shelter Early, pick up the supply bag, and place it on the marked spot. Restart the oxygen generator using the saved power.
3. **45–65 seconds:** Open the broken recycler. Preview Standard Repair: 3 bottles become 1 over 2 turns. Confirm and watch the bottles. Open Compare ISS Recovery: 14 of 20 at historical 70%, 17 at historical 85%. Both go to Further Treatment; the crew inventory stays unchanged. Close.
4. **65–80 seconds:** Continue, Sort and Store Plastic, confirm and continue to sunrise. Open the antenna, Send Now, confirm and continue. Point out the decision summary and **“Signal sent. Waiting for confirmation.”** Earth receipt is unresolved.
5. **80–90 seconds:** Explain that Water Training, available from a fresh Start screen, repeats the fixed 3-bottle checkpoint. With the kit: 1 turn, 2 bottles left. Standard tools: 2 turns, 1 bottle left. Videos can be added later; gameplay already works with text fallbacks.

Use the tray shortcuts or the matching glowing devices. Every choice has a separate Confirm action. If demonstrating a mistake, leaving the heat valve unchanged leads to a friendly recovery and checkpoint retry.

The route is a speaking guide, not a timed gameplay requirement. Allow extra time for audience questions and keyboard navigation.
